Summary of Google's WikiSkill research: three-layer knowledge management for AI agents — read-only raw logs, append-only insights, and modifiable execution manuals. Structured manuals let smaller models (9B) outperform larger ones (27B); poor documentation degrades even top-tier models. Quote: "原始记录、提炼认知、可执行手册是三码事，把失败变成资产，学习的飞轮才会真正转起来" (raw records / refined insight / executable manual are three different things — turn failure into an asset for the learning flywheel to actually spin).
